# Remote work Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out the rules on remote work at Vandenberg Logistics NV.

## Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. At least 2 office days are required, including the Tuesday team anchor day.

## Conditions

Working from abroad needs manager approval and is limited to 10 days per calendar year.

## Validity

Version 3.0, effective 2025-01-01. Supersedes version 1.2 (2022). Approved by the Management Committee.

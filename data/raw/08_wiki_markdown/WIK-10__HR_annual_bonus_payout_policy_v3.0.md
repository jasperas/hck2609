# Annual bonus payout Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out the rules on annual bonus payout at Vandenberg Logistics NV.

## Policy

The annual bonus is paid with the March payroll.

## Conditions

Employees must be in active service on 31 December of the performance year.

Version 3.0, effective 2025-01-01. Supersedes version 1.2 (2022). Approved by the Management Committee.

# Company car policy Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out the rules on company car policy at Vandenberg Logistics NV.

## Policy

New lease contracts are limited to fully electric vehicles with a list price up to EUR 55,000.

## Conditions

Existing lease contracts continue until their contractual end date.

## Validity

Version 3.0, effective 2025-01-01. Supersedes version 1.2 (2022). Approved by the Management Committee.

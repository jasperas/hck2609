# Parental leave Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out the rules on parental leave at Vandenberg Logistics NV.

## Policy

Parental leave is 16 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver and 8 weeks fully paid for the secondary caregiver.

## Conditions

Eligibility requires at least 6 months of service on the start date of the leave.
